WeFind

A Location-Based Boarding House Finder Mobile Application

Finding a boarding house usually means walking around, asking people, and calling landlords one by one. WeFind puts available rooms in Cotabato City on a map, so tenants can compare places, talk to landlords, and reserve a room from their phone, while landlords manage listings and tenants in one place.

RoleGroup leader and lead developer
TypeCapstone project, 2026
PlatformsAndroid app and web admin panel

Key features

  • Map-based search with Google Maps and a location picker limited to Cotabato City
  • Tenant and landlord accounts with their own screens, listings, and saved places
  • Chat and voice calls with photo sharing and an incoming-call screen
  • Reservations with GCash payment proof, landlord confirmation, and contracts
  • Reviews and ratings kept accurate with Firestore transactions
  • Admin panel for landlord verification, reports, and approval emails
  • Shareable links that open listings in the app with Android App Links
  • In-app updates that prompt users when a new version is out
